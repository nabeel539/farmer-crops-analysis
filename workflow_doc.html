1. Executive Overview & Purpose

Krishi AgriTech is an integrated digital agronomy platform designed to digitize farm parcel boundaries, track chronological wheat phenology, empower farmers with real-time micro-climate intelligence, and provide central management with satellite-driven ground truth visibility.

🚜 Farmer Portal (/farmer)

Self-service mobile/web portal for daily activity logging (Date/Time), spray advisories, disease detection, and live mandi rates.

🏛️ Admin Center (/admin)

GIS Command Center with interactive satellite map, farmer parcel fly-to zoom, enrollment verification, and activity feeds.

🔍 Field Officer (/field-officer)

Ground auditing interface for cadastral boundary validation, pest threshold checks, and agronomist feedback.

2. Step-by-Step Operational Lifecycle Workflow
1

Farmer Enrollment & KYC Verification (/admin/farmers)

Central administration registers the grower's legal identity (Aadhaar/CNIC, Contact, Village, and Total Landholding). The grower is assigned an official code (e.g. FARM-2026-001 for Sardar Gurpreet Singh).

2

Satellite Land Parcel Geofencing (/admin/land-parcels)

Using the interactive Satellite Location Picker, officers drop a draggable GPS pin or use live geolocation to map exact cadastral coordinates (e.g. Khasra #412/1, 18.0 Acres) with real-time acreage polygon computation.

3

Crop Cycle Initiation & Phenology Monitoring (/admin/crop-cycles)

The wheat cycle (HD-2967) is tracked across 11 agronomic stages (Sowing ➔ CRI ➔ Tillering ➔ Jointing ➔ Booting ➔ Heading/Flowering ➔ Maturity). Sentinel-2 NDVI canopy vigour (0.86 Optimal) is continuously monitored.

4

Daily Field Operations & Activity Logging (/farmer)

Growers open the "+ Record Activity" modal with exact Date & Time inputs to log operations like Urea Top-Dressing (1 Bag/Acre = 18 Bags (900 Kg) Total, ₹4,800 cost) or Canal Irrigation, instantly syncing to the central audit feed.

5

Micro-Climate Weather & Smart Spray Matrix (/farmer)

Live agro-meteorological telemetry displays temperature (27°C), humidity (55%), root-zone moisture (64%), and calculates a Safe Spray Window (07:00 AM – 11:30 AM) based on wind velocity (<10 km/h) and 0% rain chance.

6

AI Crop Doctor & Pest Early Warning

Growers capture leaf images to run on-device computer vision checks for Stripe/Yellow Rust (Puccinia striiformis) or connect directly with the Punjab Agromet Helpline (0800-15000).

7

Harvest Settlement & Mandi Procurement (/admin/harvest)

Yield estimation (54.5 Mnds/Acre) is verified at harvest. Live grain pricing compares Govt MSP (₹2,275/Qtl) vs Khanna Mandi (₹2,420/Qtl) vs Silo Gate Rates (₹2,480/Qtl for <11% moisture).

3. Data Entities & Architecture
Entity Key Attributes Description & Role
Farmer id, fullName, cnic, phone, village, totalLandAcres Verified grower identity, landholding summary, contact records.
LandParcel id, farmerId, parcelCode, khasraNo, acreage, lat, lng Cadastral plot geometry with satellite overlay coordinates.
CropCycle id, cropVariety, season, currentStage, ndviScore, yield Lifecycle progress from sowing to harvest with canopy vigour.
FieldActivity id, farmerId, activityType, executedDate, dosage, cost Timestamped log of fertilizers (Urea/DAP), water, and chemicals.
WeatherDay date, tempMax, tempMin, windSpeed, rainChance, advice Micro-climate forecasts and automated agronomic recommendations.
